Hey, I'm Britany. Staff product designer at Intuit QuickBooks, Payments team.

      
I've spent my career across fintech, healthcare, energy, and enterprise software. The most durable products come from teams genuinely excited about the problem they're solving.

      
I obsess over user needs. I read support tickets for fun. A designer's job is to be the loudest voice in the room for the person who isn't in the room.

      
I'm looking for a team that finds joy in the work and genuinely cares about the people using what they build.

      
When I'm not designing, I'm listening to an audiobook, working on pottery, or taking ridiculously long walks through Wisconsin.
